What this model uses

Lake latitude, elevation, approximate basin/depth class and size establish the current beta climatology. Live NWS forecasts are used for U.S. lakes; ECCC PROGNOS GDPS is queried for Canadian lakes. NASA GIBS supplies MODIS/VIIRS true-color and NDSI imagery. During the active spring season, near-term thaw-degree accumulation shifts the lake’s modeled breakup window. Lake-specific historical calibration and automatic satellite open-water classification are intentionally not represented as complete yet.
Not an ice-safety tool. Ice-out probability does not estimate ice thickness or whether ice can support people, vehicles or equipment.
