How this forecast is built

Lake names come from official U.S. GNIS and Canadian CGNDB sources. U.S. search is backed by the local static lake index so common names remain searchable and can be separated by county and state.

When a lake has an unambiguous HydroLAKES match, the model adds lake area, estimated mean depth, elevation, shoreline development and volume. Historical ice-out observations are used directly where a safe match exists; otherwise nearby long-record lakes provide regional calibration.

During active spring breakup, validated seasonal freezing/thaw physics and operational weather signals can adjust the historical baseline. NASA GIBS imagery remains visual evidence rather than a fabricated automatic open-water percentage.

Not an ice-safety tool. Ice-out probability does not estimate ice thickness or whether ice can support people, vehicles or equipment.
