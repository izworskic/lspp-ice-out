What this model uses

Search now reaches official U.S. GNIS and Canadian CGNDB lake names instead of a fixed list. When a newly selected lake has an unambiguous match in the derived HydroLAKES index, the model automatically adds lake area, estimated mean depth, elevation, shoreline development and volume; ambiguous matches remain broad and low-confidence rather than being guessed. NASA GIBS supplies MODIS/VIIRS true-color and NDSI imagery, while live NWS/ECCC forecasts provide thaw-degree guidance during active spring breakup. Lake-specific historical ice-out calibration and automatic satellite open-water classification remain the next major model layers.
Not an ice-safety tool. Ice-out probability does not estimate ice thickness or whether ice can support people, vehicles or equipment.
